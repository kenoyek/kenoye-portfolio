# Case Study Asset Needs

Date: 2026-07-24

The Milestone 3 case-study system does not generate or simulate screenshots. Missing product media is documented here and rendered as text requirements rather than broken images.

## Asele

- Priority: high.
- Needed: sanitized web dashboard, mobile wellness/cycle workflow, reminder or notification context, administrative workflow.
- Recommended dimensions: 1600 x 1200 for desktop/product screenshots; 1170 x 2532 for mobile screenshots.
- Alt text: describe the exact workflow shown without exposing personal health data.
- Redaction: remove personal health information, emails, phone numbers, IDs, internal notes, and private analytics.

## TeachEasy

- Priority: high.
- Needed: academy dashboard, scheduling workflow, tutor classroom, student workspace, tenant branding or permissions diagram.
- Recommended dimensions: 1600 x 1000 for web screenshots.
- Alt text: name the portal and workflow, such as "Tutor portal showing class schedule."
- Redaction: remove student names, parent data, tutor contact details, payment details, and tenant-private information.

## Reframe

- Priority: high.
- Needed: mobile news feed, article reader, community feed, deep-link or push-notification workflow if safe to show.
- Recommended dimensions: 1170 x 2532 for mobile screenshots.
- Alt text: describe the screen and platform context.
- Redaction: avoid private moderation data, unpublished editorial content, credentials, or user-identifying information.

## Shared Social Media

- Priority: medium.
- Needed: project-specific Open Graph fallback or one high-quality portfolio-wide social image.
- Recommended dimensions: 1200 x 630.
- Guidance: use accurate project titles and role language; avoid logos or app screens unless usage rights and privacy are confirmed.
